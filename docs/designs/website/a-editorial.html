01 / The problem

Benchmarks leak their labels.

Fake voices are MP3, real ones AAC. Face swaps are MPEG-4. A model can learn the format.

02 / Our fix

Clean every clip the same way.

One codec, 3 seconds from the first sound, one loudness. Then check that the clues are gone.

03 / What still fails

New generators beat lip-sync.

Sora 2 and Veo 3.1 make sound and picture together, so the lips already match.
