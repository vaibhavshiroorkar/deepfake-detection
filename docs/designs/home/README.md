# Home page designs (11-20)

Three light designs for the new home page, in the spirit of mobbin.com, for Vaibhav to choose from. Each is one
long page that tells the story from a visitor's side (what it does, how to use it, what the answer means, what it
misses, privacy); training and results stay on the Research page. The product is drawn with the real numbers of
the Veo 3.1 test clip (Overall 0.96, Face 0.96, Veo 3.1 71%) and the live catch rates.

- [a-gallery.html](a-gallery.html): closest to Mobbin. White page, black type, the product in soft grey rounded
  frames, everything centred.
- [b-soft-shapes.html](b-soft-shapes.html): a pale cool page; the three possible answers fanned as cards over
  pastel shapes; a dark closing panel.
- [c-presentation.html](c-presentation.html): the home page as the exhibition talk. Each section fills the screen
  and snaps into place on scroll, with a progress rail; left-aligned copy, the product on the right.

View them with any local web server, for example `python -m http.server` in this folder.
