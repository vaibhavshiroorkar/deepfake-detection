## 6. Protocol of the new evaluations (fixed before any score was computed)

1. Manifests and groups built from metadata only by scripts/posteval/build_manifests.py, written once (the script refuses to overwrite): data/_manifests/posteval/e033_lavdf.{manifest.csv, exclusions.csv, groups.json} and e034_cfeval.{shards.json, manifest.csv, groups.json}.
2. Hypotheses, success and failure lines, predictions, metrics and outputs written into docs/EXPERIMENTS.md (E-033, E-034) and hashed into data/_results/posteval/<E>/prereg.json (2026-10-31 01:20:02 +0530) before the first clip was decoded or the first shard downloaded.
3. Scoring with the product adapter, one sample at a time, labels never read by the scorer; predictions written once (resume only appends).
4. Exact duplicate checks by SHA-256 before metrics; duplicates excluded and listed.
5. Score files (split post_x) and descriptors exported to data/_evallab/imports/posteval/; metrics computed through the Evaluation Lab itself with the cluster bootstrap of the project (2,000 resamples, seed 20261031).
6. Nothing selected, tuned or changed on the results.

Details and the reasoning behind every choice: docs/POST_FREEZE_EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md.
