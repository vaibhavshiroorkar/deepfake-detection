How to read the result

The model is EfficientNet-B0 with a GRU over 16 face crops, trained on FakeAVCeleb. It answers "fake" at a score of 0.5 or above.

Before it answers, a familiarity check compares the clip with the genuine footage the model was trained on. When the clip looks unlike that footage, the demo withholds the verdict. Most clips from phones, films or the web will be withheld, and that is the expected result.
