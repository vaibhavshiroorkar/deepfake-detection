The four answers

Signs of manipulation
A score reached 0.6 or more (0 is real, 0.5 neutral, 1 fake). It names which model.
Can't tell
The highest score is between 0.4 and 0.6: neutral.
No signs found
Every score is below 0.4. Not proof the video is real.
Can't check
No steady face, no speech, or the clip broke a rule. It says which.
