Is this video real?

Upload a clip of one person talking. The site checks the face, the voice and the lip movement, then shows what it found and how far you can trust that.

What gets checked

Each check gives its own score, and the result shows all three.

Face

Looks for the blending and texture that face swaps leave, frame by frame.

Voice

Listens for the patterns of cloned or synthetic speech.

Lip sync

Checks whether the mouth moves with the sound. It was trained on real clips only.

Limits

  • "No signs found" is not proof a video is real. On footage unlike the training data, the checks miss many fakes.
  • About 5 in 100 real clips get flagged. That rate was measured on four research datasets; for anything else it is an estimate.
  • It needs one face and speech. Other clips get "can't check".
