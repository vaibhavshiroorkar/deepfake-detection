Is this video real?

A final-year research project on audio-visual deepfakes. Three trained models check the face, the voice and the lip movement of a talking-head video, and the site says what they found and how far you can trust it.

Check a video How it works

How it works

1. Give it a video

Upload a clip of one person talking, or record one with your camera or microphone. A voice recording or a picture works too.

2. Three models check it

One looks at the face, one listens to the voice, and one checks whether the lips move with the words. The whole video is checked, three seconds at a time.

3. You get a plain answer

Signs of manipulation, can't tell, or no signs found, with each model's score and a timeline showing which moment stood out.

Your video stays private

Your video is checked on our computer and deleted before you see the answer. Nothing is kept or shared.

Limits

  • "No signs found" is not proof a video is real. On footage unlike the training data, the checks miss many fakes.
  • About 5 in 100 real clips get flagged. That rate was measured on four research datasets; for anything else it is an estimate.
  • It needs one face and speech. Other clips get "can't check".
