Step 1 of 4

Clean the file.

Research datasets give away their labels through the file: format, length, silence. Every clip is rewritten the same way, so the checks must look at the face and the voice.

Step 2 of 4

Run four checks.

Each looks for a different kind of fake. A label an AI tool wrote into the file counts too.

FaceSwapped faces, from 16 face crops
GeneratedFaces made by AI video tools
VoiceCloned or synthetic speech
Lip syncMouth and sound out of step
File auditAI labels, such as C2PA

Lip sync: the mouth against the sound.

The mouth opens as speech gets loud. In a dubbed clip the two drift apart. This check learned from real clips only.

Step 3 of 4

Compare with real clips.

A raw score means little. Each one is placed among thousands of real clips. A long video is checked in 3-second parts, and its most unusual part decides.

Face97

Higher than 97 in 100 real clips.

Step 4 of 4

Give the answer.
