Does audio-visual fusion make deepfake detection generalize?

Final project report, built {{ today }} from the evaluation files under runs/.

Question. Do detectors that combine face, voice and lip-sync evidence hold up better on footage from other sources than a face-only detector?

Answer: no. Trained on FakeAVCeleb, every model is near perfect on unseen speakers from the same corpus (ROC-AUC 0.998 to 0.9994). On DFDC, footage they never saw, fusion never beat its own visual branch (-0.0085, -0.0215 and -0.0417 at three seeds). The five-stream fusion this app serves reads 0.5488.

Best model. An equal-weight average of six face models (two backbones, three seeds each) reaches {{ "%.4f"|format(r.ensemble.roc_auc) }} [{{ "%.3f"|format(r.ensemble.ci[0]) }}, {{ "%.3f"|format(r.ensemble.ci[1]) }}] on DFDC and 0.9994 in-domain. That is +0.047 over the average single seed, a gain clear of zero. Against the luckiest seed it is +0.013, not clear of zero. The gain does not transfer: on Celeb-DF it reads {{ "%.4f"|format(r.ensemble.celebdf.roc_auc) }}, level with the seed-17 model, and on MNW at 0.5 it catches {{ r.ensemble.mnw.ensemble }} of {{ r.ensemble.mnw.fakes }} fakes against {{ r.ensemble.mnw.best_single }}. One member, ConvNeXt seed 43, diverged in training (F29); leaving it out lowers DFDC to 0.7666. The ensemble ships as the web app's default for its DFDC ranking, with those limits stated.

What the project adds. Mostly the checks: an attention metric that showed the cross-modal streams ignore lip-sync; a benchmark confound found before training; seed variance larger than any effect reported; and corruption that destroys the decision while ROC-AUC barely moves.

2. Method and math

Each clip is decoded into 16 frames. MTCNN finds the face, a tracker keeps one identity, and crops are resized to 224 pixels. The audio is resampled to 16 kHz with leading silence removed. Design A trains three branches (visual, audio, sync) and fuses them with a logistic head fitted on out-of-fold scores. Design B trains five streams, two of them audio-to-mouth cross-attention, and fuses their 256-dimensional embeddings.
